Privacy Policy

Friends of Kristin McCabe  ·  Fulton County School Board Campaign

Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines our practices for the online collection, use, and disclosure of information you provide to Friends of Kristin McCabe ("we," "us," or "our") when you use this website and engage with our services.

By using this site, you agree that this Privacy Policy governs your use of the site. From time to time we may update this Privacy Policy. We encourage you to periodically check this page for updates. Your continued use of the site affirms your agreement to any changes we make to this Privacy Policy.

Information Collected

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us as well as certain information generated automatically by your use of the site.

Voluntary Information

We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, and postal address. You may provide this information by filling out forms, requesting information, registering as a supporter, or for other campaign-related purposes. We may also collect demographic information such as zip code and contact preferences. In limited circumstances, we may collect payment information where needed to complete a requested transaction, such as a campaign donation.

Automatically Generated Information

As you navigate through and interact with our site, we and our service providers may use automatic data collection technologies to collect non-personally identifiable information generated automatically while you visit the site. This may include web pages visited before and after visiting our site, date and time, server logs, domain type, device type, links you click within the site, and other communication data regarding resources you access. We may also collect your IP address, which is a unique identifier that electronic devices use to communicate with each other on the Internet.

How It Is Collected

We collect information directly from you when you provide it to us, and from third parties such as service providers and business partners who help us operate our campaign and website.

Cookies

We may use "cookies" — small pieces of information stored by your browser — to enhance your experience on our site. You may refuse to accept browser cookies by activating the appropriate setting on your browser. However, doing so may limit your ability to access certain parts of the site. Unless you have adjusted your browser setting to refuse cookies, our system may issue cookies when you visit our site.

Web Beacons

Pages of our site and our emails may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that allow us to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email and to gather related website statistics, such as recording the popularity of certain content and verifying system integrity.

Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information about your use of the site (including your IP address) and transmits it to Google. This information is used to evaluate visitors' use of the website and compile statistical reports. Google Analytics collects information anonymously and will not associate your IP address with any other data held by Google. We will not associate any data gathered from this site with any personally identifiable information unless you explicitly submit that information via a form on our website. You may opt out of Google Analytics by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-On.

You may also opt out by contacting us at: Friends of Kristin McCabe.

How We Use Your Information

Information we collect may be used to communicate with you about the campaign, send updates and volunteer opportunities, process donations, improve our website, and comply with applicable laws. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Children's Online Privacy Protection Act Compliance

We comply with the requirements of COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act). We do not knowingly collect or maintain personal information from anyone under 13 years of age. Our website, information, and services are directed to people who are at least 13 years of age or older.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact: Friends of Kristin McCabe, Johns Creek, Georgia.
